ACTIVE SCAM ALERT

Don't share OTPs or UPI PINs

No bank, official, or support team will ask for these details. A request to “receive money” through a UPI PIN is a scam.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers for a safer, simpler reporting process.

Still unsure? Satya can help you understand the next right step.

What should I keep ready before filing a report?

Keep screenshots, emails or messages, website or profile links, phone numbers, transaction IDs, dates, times, and the amount involved. Do not delete the original evidence.

What happens after I submit a complaint?

You receive a reference number. Your complaint is securely recorded and reviewed by the relevant authorities. Use the tracker to see important status updates.

Can I report a scam even if I have not lost money?

Yes. Report phishing, impersonation, threatening content, and suspicious accounts or links. Early reports can help protect you and others.

How can I protect myself after a suspected financial fraud?

Call 1930 promptly, contact your bank through its official number, change affected passwords, and avoid sharing OTPs, UPI PINs, or screen access with anyone.

Will my personal information remain private?

Your details are used only to help process your complaint and are handled with privacy-focused safeguards. Review your draft carefully before submitting.
